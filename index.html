The Arcane Ledger

Scrying your fantasy league for truths your group chat refuses to hear

Summon your league

Pull a league straight from Sleeper, or paste in any league's weekly scores. The Ledger does the rest.

Find it in the Sleeper URL for your league. Regular-season weeks with scores are pulled automatically. Past seasons work too, each season has its own ID.

One line per team per week: week,team,points,opponent. Tabs work too, so you can paste straight from a spreadsheet. Team names can't contain commas.

The Oracle

Seraphina, Keeper of the Ledger, narrates your season. Press play (sound on for the full effect).

Voices come from your device, so the list depends on what it has installed. On iPhone, Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices lets you download better ones, and they show up here. Your pick is remembered.

Every metric, every house. Tap a column to sort. Hover (or long-press) a header for what it means.

Mana efficiency: points versus wins

Floor and ceiling: the volatility of every house

Faint bar is worst to best week. Gold band is the average of the three worst to three best weeks. The white tick is the average.

Weekly heat: where each score ranked that week

Gold means top of the league that week, violet means bottom. A green or red dot marks the actual result.
